Central Nitrogen atom covalently bonded to three Oxygen atoms. The entire molecular framework gains 1 electron, delocalised via resonance across oxygen bonds.

Tetraoxosulphate (VI) Ion SO₄²⁻

Central Sulphur atom shares electrons with four Oxygen atoms. The cluster accepts 2 extra electrons to satisfy all outer octets, resulting in a stable doubly-charged anion.

Net Charge: -2 | Valency: 2
Trioxocarbonate (IV) Ion CO₃²⁻

Carbon bonded to three Oxygen atoms in a trigonal planar arrangement. Captures 2 extra electrons to stabilise formal charges across oxygen atoms.

Ammonium Ion NH₄⁺

Nitrogen shares its lone pair with an incoming H⁺ proton (dative covalent bond). The entire molecule loses 1 electron relative to total protons, creating a polyatomic cation.

Net Charge: +1 | Valency: 1

Central Phosphorus atom surrounded by four Oxygen atoms. The polyatomic cluster accepts 3 electrons to complete oxygen octets.
