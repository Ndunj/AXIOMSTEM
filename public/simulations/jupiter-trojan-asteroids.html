Jupiter & Trojan Asteroids Dynamics

Jupiter Trojan System

Jupiter's Trojans are two large swarms of rocky asteroids sharing Jupiter's orbit around the Sun. They remain trapped in stable gravitational pockets known as Lagrange points.

Jupiter's Galilean Moons

Jupiter features its 4 primary natural satellites orbiting along its equator:

  • Io: Volcanically active inner moon
  • Europa: Ice-covered ocean world
  • Ganymede: The largest moon in the Solar System
  • Callisto: Heavily cratered ancient body
Sun: Opaque glowing golden sphere
Jupiter & Moons: High-visibility moons Io, Europa, Ganymede & Callisto
L4 Greek Camp: Carbonaceous rocky asteroids (60° ahead)
L5 Trojan Camp: Reddish-brown silicate asteroids (60° behind)

Lagrange Points (L4 & L5)

The gravitational forces balance with centrifugal force at these points, located 60° ahead of and behind Jupiter along its orbital plane.
