Kepler's Three Laws

1. Law of Ellipses
The orbit of a planet is an ellipse with the Sun at one of the two foci ($F_1$).
2. Law of Equal Areas
A line segment joining a planet and the Sun sweeps out equal areas during equal intervals of time ($dA/dt = L / 2m = \text{const}$).
3. Harmonic Law ($T^2 = a^3$)
The square of the orbital period ($T^2$) is directly proportional to the cube of the semi-major axis ($a^3$).
