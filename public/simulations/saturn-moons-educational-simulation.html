Key Characteristics
The Ring System

Saturn's defining feature is its extensive ring system, composed mostly of water ice, rocky debris, and dust particles. These rings span tens of thousands of kilometers yet are remarkably thin—often less than a kilometer thick.

Moons & Satellites

Saturn hosts a diverse system of over 100 moons. Visible in this simulation:

  • Mimas
  • Enceladus
  • Tethys
  • Dione
  • Rhea
  • Titan
  • Iapetus
Atmospheric Features

Saturn displays faint atmospheric banding caused by high-speed winds exceeding 1,800 km/h, alongside a unique hexagonal jet stream surrounding its north pole.

Density

Saturn is the least dense planet in the Solar System, with an average density lower than that of water.
