📖 Core Definitions

1. Spacetime Grid

The 2D blue sheet embeds a 3D visual of 4D spacetime. Mass causes this grid to stretch and dip downward.

2. Geodesics (Orbital Paths)

Objects don't feel a pulling "force". They travel in straight paths across curved space, appearing to orbit.

3. Event Horizon

The bright cyan ring marks the radius where escape velocity equals light speed (v_esc = c). Nothing escapes.

The Central Black Hole Structure

  • The Black Sphere (Singularity & Horizon): Represents the region of space where gravity is so strong that no light or matter can escape once inside.
  • The Bright Cyan Ring (Event Horizon Boundary): Represents the mathematical boundary around the black hole where the escape velocity exactly equals the speed of light (v_esc = c).
  • Orbital Behavior: Outside this ring, objects can maintain stable orbits indefinitely. Once an object crosses inside it, all paths lead inward toward the singularity.
